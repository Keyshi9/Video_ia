The most famous jail ever stormed was really a gunpowder run.
Paris, July fourteenth, seventeen eighty-nine.
That morning, the crowd grabbed about thirty thousand muskets. No powder.
The powder? Two hundred and fifty barrels, moved into the Bastille days before. For safekeeping.
About four hours of shooting later, the governor gave up.
Luckily, the prisoners were freed.
Unluckily, there were seven. Four forgers, two madmen, and a count locked up by his own family.
The crowd paraded them, then went back to the barrels.
The Marquis de Sade missed it. He'd yelled to passers-by that prisoners were being massacred, so they'd moved him out.
